EUMS University, located in Brest in western France, is a highly regarded institution known for excellence in teaching and research. It brings together nine faculties, a central research hub, and a graduate school to provide a world-class learning experience.

Diverse Academic Programs

Our faculties span arts & humanities, science & technology, law & economics, and the social sciences. From bachelor’s to master’s and PhD, students learn from distinguished scholars while gaining practical research experience.

Innovative Research

The research center hosts specialized institutes focused on real-world challenges. Cross-disciplinary collaboration fuels innovation and encourages the exchange of ideas across fields.

Graduate Excellence

The graduate school offers advanced degrees across many disciplines with strong mentorship and resources. Students build expertise, pursue cutting-edge research, and graduate ready to lead in their careers.
