About the University of Brest

The University of Brest is a leading academic institution in western France, renowned for excellence in teaching and research across many disciplines. It brings together nine faculties, a central research center, and a graduate school.

Programs span arts & humanities, science & technology, law & economics, and social sciences. Beyond bachelor’s degrees, master’s and doctoral paths enable advanced scholarship and impactful research with distinguished faculty.
